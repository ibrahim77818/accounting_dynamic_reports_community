Dynamic Financial Reports

Eleven interactive accounting reports for Odoo 16 Community: Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss, Cash Flow, Executive Summary, ledgers, Trial Balance, aged balances, journals and tax. Filter, compare periods and drill down on screen, then export a colour-coded PDF or Excel file in one click.

Why this module

Odoo 16 Community posts every journal entry correctly but gives you almost no way to read them back as financial statements.

In standard Odoo 16 Community

  • No Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss or Cash Flow report: those are Enterprise only.
  • No General Ledger, Trial Balance or Partner Ledger: you group journal items by hand.
  • No Aged Receivable / Payable with ageing buckets.
  • No period comparison side by side.
  • Exports are flat lists, with no formatted PDF for management or auditors.
  • Negative balances look exactly like positive ones.

With this module

  • Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss, Cash Flow Statement and an Executive Summary, on top of Community.
  • General Ledger, Trial Balance, Partner Ledger and Consolidated Journals, with drill-down to Journal Items.
  • Aged Receivable and Aged Payable in seven buckets, per partner and per invoice.
  • Compare with any number of previous periods or a custom period.
  • One-click PDF (modern layout, header and column heads never overlap) and Excel.
  • Colour coding everywhere: negatives in red, zeros as a grey dash, totals on a navy band.

How it works

Nothing to configure: install it and open a report.

1
Open
Go to Invoicing › Reporting › Dynamic Reports and pick a report.
2
Filter
Choose the date or period, comparison, journals, accounts, analytic accounts or partners.
3
Explore
Expand sections and accounts, and jump to the journal items behind any figure.
4
Export
Click PDF or Excel, or the envelope to e-mail the PDF to yourself.

Features

Screenshots use a demo company with two years of activity: about 70 customer invoices, vendor bills and refunds, full and partial payments, draft entries, two analytic projects and a misc. reversal entry.

Menus

All reports in one place

A new Dynamic Reports section under Invoicing › Reporting (or Accounting › Reporting) lists the 11 reports. It is visible to Billing users and to the Read-only accounting role, so it works in a plain Community database.

Feature 1

Balance Sheet that always balances

ASSETS, LIABILITIES and EQUITY broken down into Odoo’s account types, with Current Year Earnings computed automatically. Click a section to expand it down to each account; the EQUITY + LIABILITIES line sits on a navy band so you can check it against total assets at a glance.

Feature 2

Profit and Loss with period comparison

Gross Profit, Total Income, Expense and Net Profit, each down to the account. Open Compare and set Previous periods (here 2), or a custom start and end date, to get one column per period next to the current one.

Rule: Net Profit = Total Income − Expense, for every column. In the screenshot, $ 232,937.00 − $ 105,606.60 = $ 127,330.40 for the first comparison period.
Feature 3

Cash Flow Statement and Executive Summary

The Cash Flow Statement shows cash at the beginning of the period, the net increase and the closing balance. The Executive Summary groups key figures by Cash, Balance Sheet, Profitability, Position and Performance (margins, return on investments, average debtor and creditor days).

Feature 4

General Ledger down to every journal item

One line per account with debit, credit and balance. Click an account to load its journal items (date, journal, partner, entry, label, running balance). The ⋮ menu on a line opens the Move Line in Odoo. Large accounts load in pages with Load More.

Feature 5

Trial Balance with initial, period and ending balances

Debit, credit and balance for the Initial Balance, the selected period and the Ending Balance, with a total row. From any account, the ⋮ menu opens its Journal Items or its General Ledger.

Feature 6

Partner Ledger and Aged Receivable / Payable

The Partner Ledger shows each customer and vendor with debit, credit and balance; filter on Receivable or Payable, and on reconciled or unreconciled entries. Aged Receivable and Aged Payable spread open amounts over Not Due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–120, 121–180 and 180+ days, per partner and per invoice.

Feature 7

Tax Report and Consolidated Journals

The Tax Report lists the net amount and tax for every sales and purchase tax, and can be compared over several periods. Consolidated Journals gives debit, credit and balance per journal, then per account inside each journal.

Feature 8

Filters that stay on screen

Every filter is a button in the control panel that shows its current value. Dates: Today, End of Last Month / Quarter / Financial Year, or This / Last Month, Quarter, Financial Year for period reports, plus a Custom date. Journals (single journals or journal groups), Accounts with a search box, Analytic and Partners with type-ahead search. Options switches between Posted and Draft entries, a date range, and Print with details.

Feature 9

Modern PDF that never overlaps

Every PDF has the company logo and name, the report title and period, the print date and Page X / Y, then a summary of the active filters. The navy column header repeats on every page, under a header band of fixed height, so it is never drawn over the header or the rows. Statements print in portrait; ledgers, the Trial Balance and aged reports in landscape.

Page 2 of a 13-page General Ledger: the page header, the repeated column header and the first row are cleanly separated.
Feature 10

Excel ready to share

The Excel button exports the report as it is filtered on screen: navy column headers, highlighted totals, thousands separators, negatives in red, and sheets already set to print on A4 landscape, one page wide.

Feature 11

Four settings, all optional

In Settings › Invoicing › Dynamic Financial Reports: Enable Ledger Initial Balance (General and Partner Ledger), Disable Trial Initial Balance (moves income and expense initial balances to Current Year Earnings), Disable Balance Sheet Sign and Enable Net Tax. Account types used by the reports can be reviewed in Configuration › Accounting › Account Type.

One colour language, three outputs

The browser, the PDF and the Excel file use the same rules, so a figure reads the same wherever it is printed.

-$ 9,944.87 Negative amount: red
- Zero: a grey dash instead of 0.00
Section Report sections and account groups: tinted row, bold
Net Profit Grand totals: navy band, positive in green, negative in light red
Amounts keep your currency and its position (before or after the number) and use your language’s thousands separator.

Built for real-world corner cases

Each of these is covered by the automated tests or by the filter matrix run before release.

Several companies
Reports read only the companies selected in the company switcher; another company’s entries never leak into the totals.
Draft entries
Show posted entries only, draft only, or both. Cancelled entries are always excluded.
Partial payments
Aged reports use the open residual of each invoice, so a half-paid invoice appears with its remaining amount only.
Refunds and reversals
Credit notes and reversal entries reduce the right accounts and show in red when a balance turns negative.
Comparison periods
Income and net profit are computed separately for every comparison column and checked against the journal items.
Long ledgers
Journal items load in pages on screen. In the PDF, rows never split across pages and the column header repeats on every page.
Analytic accounting
Filter on analytic accounts through Odoo 16’s analytic distribution. The filter appears only for users with analytic accounting.
No chart of accounts yet
Installs on a fresh database without demo data and uninstalls cleanly, leaving no menus or tables behind.
Billing-only users
Works for users who only have the Invoicing rights, without “Show Full Accounting Features”.

Security

Built on Odoo’s accounting groups; journal items are always read through Odoo’s record rules.
